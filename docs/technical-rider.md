# Technical Rider

**(S)wipable, (Sc)rollable, Flushable: An Interaction Model for Toilet Paper** — Meijie Hu — TEI '27 Arts & Performances, Lisbon, 24–27 January 2027

## Space

- **Floor space:** approx. 3 × 3 m, including the toilet and room for one visitor
- **Wall space:** approx. 2 m wide, must be mountable — the holder fixes with two screws at 700 mm above floor
- **Ceiling height:** no requirement

## Lighting

- Standard top-down gallery lighting. No special requirement; the work has no visual output

## Connectivity

- **Power:** 1 standard outlet within 2 m of the wall position
- **Internet:** 2.4 GHz wi-fi. Please provide credentials at install

## Furniture

- **Toilet, as a prop.** Does not need to have water, or be plumbed or flushable — a display, salvaged or ex-stock unit is fine
- **Toilet paper rolls:** supplied by the artist
- No tables, pedestals, seating or barriers

## Equipment

- Screws and screwdriver for mounting
- Laptop
- Optional: Speakers

## Installation time

- **Setup:** 90 minutes, artist alone. Mount the holder, position the toilet, connect to power and wi-fi, calibrate, sound check
- **Breakdown:** 45 minutes, artist alone. Unmount, make good fixings, remove everything

## Maintenance

- Approx. 30 minutes daily, by the artist: replace the roll when low, clear pulled paper, confirm connection
